About Keto Diet Plan

Keto Diet Plan is an independent food and nutrition website created to share practical, well-researched information about the ketogenic diet. Our guides are written in plain language, our tools run entirely in your browser, and we never ask you to sign up or share personal data.

We are not doctors or registered dietitians. The content here is educational and is meant to help you make informed choices, not to replace professional advice. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or take medication, please talk to a qualified health professional before changing your diet.

Every article on this site is written originally for our readers. We update guides when nutrition guidance or our own understanding changes, and we aim to keep every tool accurate and easy to use.
